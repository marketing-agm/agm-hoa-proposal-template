03 · Governance

Governance & Board Support

Structured governance support through meeting coordination, documentation management, and operational guidance. AGM attends regularly scheduled Board meetings monthly, or more frequently at the Board’s request, and will coordinate special meetings as needed to address operational matters, capital projects, or regulatory requirements.

The firm maintains familiarity with applicable provisions of the Washington Condominium Act (RCW 64.34) and Washington Uniform Common Interest Ownership Act (RCW 64.90), along with other statutes affecting condominium governance, and assists the Board in understanding its fiduciary obligations and operational responsibilities.

04A · Compliance

Interim Compliance Review

In addition to AGM’s inspections, the Board may provide AGM with identified violations by Board members, which have been approved by the Board for processing in accordance with the Association’s Compliance Policy.

Compliance inspections are performed weekly and include observation of front yards, visible side yards, exterior of home, and parking. Following the inspection, a warning letter will be issued, and the violation will then be processed in compliance with the enclosed compliance procedure.
01 · Review
Open Violation Records

Review open violation records to maintain an accurate picture of the active compliance pipeline between inspections.

02 · Verification
Compliance Status Confirmation

Following the cure period, the Board will inform AGM whether the violation remains open or the Homeowner has achieved compliance.

03 · Release
Compliance Notice

Upon approval from the Board, AGM will release approved violation notices to the Homeowner in accordance with the Compliance Policy. Assessment of fines and final enforcement decisions remain the sole responsibility of the Board.

04 · Tracking & Closure
Log Updates & Compliance Confirmation

The Board will update the violation log to reflect either confirmed compliance and closure, or that the violation remains open and is being escalated under the Compliance Policy.
