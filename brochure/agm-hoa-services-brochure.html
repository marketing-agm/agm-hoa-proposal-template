01Governance & Board Support

Organized governance, informed decisions, and continuity of records under Board direction.

AGM attends regularly scheduled Board meetings monthly, or more often at the Board’s request, and coordinates special meetings for operational matters, capital projects, or regulatory requirements.

We maintain familiarity with the Washington Condominium Act (RCW 64.34) and the Uniform Common Interest Ownership Act (RCW 64.90), and help the Board understand its fiduciary responsibilities.
