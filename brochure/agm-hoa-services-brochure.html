01  —  Governance & Board Support

Organized governance, informed decision-making, and continuity of records under Board direction.

AGM attends regularly scheduled Board meetings monthly, or more frequently at the Board’s request, and coordinates special meetings as needed to address operational matters, capital projects, or regulatory requirements.

We maintain familiarity with the Washington Condominium Act (RCW 64.34) and the Washington Uniform Common Interest Ownership Act (RCW 64.90), and help the Board understand its fiduciary and operational responsibilities.

Compliance process

Consistent, documented enforcement — with the Board in control.

Weekly compliance inspections cover front yards, visible side yards, home exteriors, and parking. Board-approved violations identified by Board members are processed under the same Compliance Policy.

01
Review

Open Violation Records

An accurate picture of the active compliance pipeline between inspections.

02
Verification

Status Confirmation

After the cure period, the Board confirms whether a violation remains open or is resolved.

03
Release

Compliance Notice

Upon Board approval, AGM releases notices to the Homeowner per the Compliance Policy.

04
Closure

Tracking & Closure

The violation log is updated to reflect confirmed compliance or escalation.

Assessment of fines and final enforcement decisions remain the sole responsibility of the Board.
